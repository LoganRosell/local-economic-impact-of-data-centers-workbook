# Status Update Log

You will use this document to provide a weekly status update in the form of an executive summary. 
Please use reverse chronological order (newest first).
Each update should include the following information:

**Progress from last week**
  
* What did you complete?
* What is still pending?
  
**Plan for next week**
  
* What are your top priorities, *that you believe can be completed in the upcoming week*?

**Evaluation of overall timeline**
  
* Are you on track to complete the next milestone on time?
* If not, how can you adjust your trajectory by speeding up your pace and/or simplifying the problem?
